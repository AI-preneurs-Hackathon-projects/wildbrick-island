## Ordered visual checklist

The exact controls below are visible in the harness panel. Changing a select resets the scene; **Reset** restores the selected case. **Fire + 20 ms** sends one fire command and advances 20 ms. Use **Advance 80 ms** repeatedly (up to 12 times for a one-second observation) to follow tracer/contact and windup. Inspect each step rather than only the final health. **Wait 2 seconds** lets cooldown/protection advance. Each clean single-shot expectation below assumes one reset and no previous shot.

1. Choose **Weapon → Saved car as carried pulse**, **Aim → Converged (15°)**, **Kit origin → Fresh / rebuilt blueprint**, **Synthetic emitter offset → Saved geometry**, **Target case → Body-forward normal range**, **Body facing → 0°**. Compare **Normal distance → 2 / 5 / 10 / 20**. Before firing inspect the carried barrel/emitter, dashed guide and hollow endpoint together. After firing inspect the first visible segment and confirmed impact; status should become **Target health 85.6**, **Confirmed hits / blocked 1 / 0**, exactly once. Corrections are 15° / 10.43° / 4.76° / 2.28°. At 2 m the immediate launch bridge already touches the rival: expect a short body-to-contact effect, not a long tracer falsely starting beyond the victim. At 5/10/20 m expect a straight outgoing ray from the posed emitter.
2. Repeat those distances with **Aim → Fixed comparison** and **Body facing → 0°**. Health stays 100, hits 0; the pulse travels beside the rival. Some diagonal facings legitimately intersect the old axis-aligned envelope. Switch back to convergence and repeat **Body facing** 45° through 315°. Use **Camera orbit → Behind character / Sideways / In front / backward** independently: the camera must not turn the body/barrel or alter hit/health results. Repeat the main distance comparison with **Saved octopus as carried pulse**. These are derived carried fixtures, not captured handheld designs.
3. At 5 m set **Synthetic emitter offset → Wide left (cap test)**, then **Wide right (cap test)**. Each saturates at absolute 15°, with health 100 and hits 0. The pose, guide and tracer must honestly miss. **Mirrored lateral origin** is feasible and should hit once (85.6). Restore **Saved geometry**. The synthetic offsets test stored-origin support, not the quality of an authored weapon.
4. At normal 5 m press **Move rival across axis** repeatedly. It cycles left 2 m, center, right 2 m, center. Focus/correction must switch deterministically between fallback and the body-axis depth; it must not chase an off-axis rival. Inspect any abrupt pose/guide change for distracting flicker. At 20 m fire, then move the rival before advancing further: the already launched tracer must retain its direction and may miss. A newly solved guide is not confirmation of the old shot. Inspect fast follow-up shots for guide blinking and inspect delayed confirmation separately in multiplayer.
5. At normal distance 5 m compare **Target case → Cover on body query only** and **Cover on muzzle ray only**. The former's small body-axis obstacle selects focus but the actual muzzle ray passes beside it and hits once (85.6); the guide/tracer must visibly avoid the wall. The latter stops the real ray at the wall, leaves health 100 and records no rival hit. **Toggle cover** removes/reintroduces that blocker; reset between fire comparisons. No shot may pass through visible solid cover. This also checks focus changes when cover enters/leaves the axis.
6. Choose **Cover before target**: the body-to-muzzle path hits cover, health 100, no rival hit, short wall impact; the guide must terminate there. **Cover after target**: nearest rival wins, health 85.6, one hit. **Body-centered Z 0.9** and **Exact overlap** should each contact once. **Protected Z 0.9** leaves health 100 with **0 / 1** confirmed hits/blocked; show contact/protection feedback, not a damage flash. **Behind Z -1** and **Out of range Z 80** must not take an ordinary forward shot. Do not interpret disappearance at range expiry as an impact.
7. Repeat the relevant contact/normal cases with **Bow**, **Saved car as carried rifle**, and **Saved dragon as carried flame**. On a single successful foot-target hit, health is respectively 78.4 / 87.4 / 93.7. Flame range is 15 m, so 20 m is a legitimate miss. Generated harness kits freeze spread to isolate geometry; the full-suite spread tests retain Arena randomness. In Practice/real Arena inspect the faint corner brackets as possible spread, not a guaranteed hit region.
8. Select **Saved mounted octopus**, **Saved flying dragon** and **Twin-gun rover**. Switching **Aim** must not alter base correction (0°), emitter or fixed barrel direction; camera orbit cannot steer them. In normal 5 m facing 0°, each saved mounted case contacts the foot target once in the harness. Select **Car center Z 4** to inspect target-mount absorption/contact and the displayed mount-health decrement without assuming a foot-health decrement. With **Punch**, test **Exact overlap**, **Behind Z -1**, and **Car center Z 4** (the distant car-center punch may be out of reach); there must be no ranged guide. Overlap punch resolves after windup once, leaving the foot target at 83.8 after its existing armor calculation.

Inspect first contact and normal 5/10 m; octopus hits leave 85.6, flame 93.7. Automated tests also cover JSON reload; do not claim this button migrates real saved room kits.
10. Use **Defeat player**, **Wait 2 seconds**, **Respawn**, **Drop item** and **Pick up item** to inspect guide removal/reappearance and protection.
